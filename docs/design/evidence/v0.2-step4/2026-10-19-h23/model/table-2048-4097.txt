and 25 at the callee; two wires a pair; the slotted schedule at W = 8 calls in
flight - four pairs - and slots of 100 us. One CPU a lead, as on a cluster.
"SYN": the most reaching one listener within a round trip; "wait": the most
connections waiting at one lead for the answer to their hello.
