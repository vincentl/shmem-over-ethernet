# Adversarial read: v0.2 step 3, Träff's reduce-scatter and allgather on the data path (the merge point's)

A fresh agent's read of `main...4b9767e` on the branch `circulant`, both parts
with Part 2 the focus, and of `4b9767e..5cbd47e` once the branch moved, run on
2026-10-07, asked to find a piece combined or copied from a slot its receiver
did not wait for, a row reused early, a write into a lead's words or lane that
the lead does not wait for before it returns, a counter another collective adds
to, and any place the C differs from the model's lead program; and to check that
every node handles a call alike, the layout, threads, splits, divergent
programs, whether each test can fail, and the README's and the design's claims.
The agent could not write files, so the report is saved here as it was handed
back; what was done with each finding is in
`docs/decisions/2026-10-07-circulant-data-path.md`, "The adversarial read".
Its probes and scripts are in `build/scratch/review-v02-step3/`, outside
version control.

**Scope.** Part way through, the coordinator asked for `4b9767e..5cbd47e` too:
`3bbff45` fixes the README's memory figures; `5cbd47e` gives each Träff's round a
window of the capacity × (ceiling + 1) / ⌈log₂ N⌉ slots of the lane, runs
`shmem.lanes.*` under `tree`, cuts the sanitizers' circulant cases to 9 PEs, and
adds a reuse phase and a threads-in-turn phase to `test-circulant-reduce.c`.

**How it was run.** VM runs in `osoe-load`, under the lock, on the debug preset
built from `git archive` of `4b9767e` and of `5cbd47e`, at most 16 logical nodes
a job. Each mutation of `internode-generic.c` built with a tag string, checked in
`libosoe.so`, the pristine file restored after each. On the host,
`circulant_model.py check` whole at `4b9767e`, model cases of the reviewer's own
at windows 2 and 3, and the chooser compiled from both commits.

**Verdict:** the protocol holds at both commits.

In source, the C is the model's lead program: who is granted and into which
field; the head, then the field, then the fenced add; the lane word put before
the first add and read at the first take; credits for the first P − W pieces,
counted against a base kept the same way; the allgather grant sent at a
reduce-scatter round's end to that round's receiver; both waits, each for all
⌈log₂ N⌉ grants. Where the C differs - pieces of up to 16 KiB cut interval by
interval, putting before taking, the clears the model leaves out, the head
written by every receiver, and `5cbd47e`'s block of W slots in place of the
model's row of W - none of the differences changes anything the model checks.
The model holds rerun whole, and at windows of 2 and 3, which its own cases
never use. Nothing run broke the C: leads lending different lanes; a team
number reused 30 times between Träff's reductions; fewer elements than nodes;
windows from 1 to 128; a block that holds the lane's release row; perturbation;
divergent counts. No piece taken from a slot its receiver did not wait for, no
row or block reused early, no write into a lead's words or lane after it
returns, no counter shared with another collective.

What is wrong is around the protocol: at `5cbd47e` one of the step's own tests
fails on every run (finding 1); the README's two-node sentence is false at both
commits (finding 2); the record has no verification section, and its mutation
table was measured before `5cbd47e` changed the window (finding 3). Fix these
three before the merge. The tests reach the lanes the grants carry only through
the order in which threads start and through timing (finding 4); after the
merge, give leads different lanes by construction.

**2. The README's two-node sentence is false at both commits** (fix before
merge; confidence high). `README.md:723` at `4b9767e` ("and at every size on
two nodes - go through a tree") and `:722-724` at `5cbd47e` ("and on two nodes
at most sizes, where the two cost about the same").
- The chooser in C, the same at both commits, takes Träff's on two nodes for
  11,486,022 of the 16,776,192 sizes from 1,025 bytes to 16 MiB on the Pi's
  link (68%): 11,483 of the 16,776 multiples of 1,000 bytes, but no power of
  two and no multiple of 16 KiB. On the grid's loopback link it takes Träff's
  for 99.5% of sizes. `shape-grid.txt` at both commits has two-node Träff's
  lines, for example `reduce 2 16385 86000 7000 116000000 4 2 1024 1
  circulant`, and 1 MiB at a capacity of 2. `test-circulant-reduce` on two
  nodes ends with "Träff's from 0 bytes, 6 calls checked as Träff's" at both
  commits (measured).
- Cause: `osoe_shape_cost` charges a partial last piece as a whole piece,
  while `osoe_shape_circulant_cost` charges Träff's its exact bytes. 16,385

  18 ns more. On two nodes both algorithms send n each way, so the choice
  follows the size's remainder modulo 16 KiB; it flips 178 times on a grid of
  sizes from 1 KiB to 64 MiB. The Pi measured four nodes only.
- Fix: say this in the README. After the merge, take the tree on two nodes, or
  charge the tree's last piece by its bytes.
- The rest of the paragraph: at `5cbd47e`, "under 4 to 16 KiB by the node
  count" is right to rounding - the cost model's crossover runs from 3.5 KiB at
  511 and 512 nodes to 15.8 KiB at three; at `4b9767e`, "8 to 16" was the
  power-of-two ladder's figure.
- Related: the record at `5cbd47e` (`:172-173`) names "16,385 bytes of the
  ladder" as the two-node case, but 1,048,589 takes Träff's too. The
  `OSOE_COLLECTIVE_FANOUT` row's "one a round of a half" now undersells a
  round's rows (choice 6).

## Checked and found sound

**The C against the model** (observed in source).

| Model step | The C |
|---|---|
| G/GA (grants) | Grants go to r − s_k into field k: the head, then the field, then a fenced add. |
| Await and GRANT check | The wait is for base + q (`:1677`); every field must name this reduction. The C clears what it read, which makes the check stricter. |
| PUTS, PUTF, PUTA | Round k's piece j goes into row (at `5cbd47e`, block) k of the receiver, slot j mod W: a put, then a fenced add on row k's counter. The lane word is put before the first add. |
| TAKE/TAKEF | The lane word is read, cleared and checked at the first take. |
| CREDQ and the base | A credit for each of the first P − W pieces taken. The sender waits for base + j − W + 1. The base advances by max(0, P − W). |
| END3 and the first-gather await | The allgather grant goes to r + s_k as the reduce-scatter round ends. The allgather waits for base + q before its first round. |
| `arrived[k]` | Arrival bases per row advance by the pieces received in either half. |

A round's sent intervals at one lead are its receiver's received intervals,
built by `blocks` from the same start and run, so P and W agree on both sides.
The allgather's sends at row k are the elements that row's reduce-scatter
received, so a senders'-lane field is read exactly when the allgather back needs
it, empty blocks included.

**QUIET** (reasoned). Every grant, credit, arrival and lane word a lead receives
is one it waits for or reads before returning. The last credit of a round is for
piece P − W − 1, which the sender's last put waits for. One lead finishing a
reduction means every lead has finished its reduce-scatter and passed its
allgather wait; block x first leaves x in x's own first allgather round; so the
grant counts cannot run ahead into the next reduction.

**Every node handles a call alike** (observed in source). `shape_of` takes its
inputs from settings checked to agree across nodes (`node.c:1174`,
`rendezvous.c`'s `agree`, including the new `tree` value), the root's link handed
to every PE (or agreed settings), the team's node count from its record, and the
call's bytes. `5cbd47e`'s window comes from the ceiling and capacity, which are
agreed, and the layout's rows from the job's node count. The fatal check at
`in_rounds:1666-1671` cannot fire for any chooser shape: q × capacity ×
⌊(ceiling + 1)/q⌋ ≤ (ceiling + 1) × capacity.

**Layout** (observed in source and computed). Each team number gets ⌈log₂ N⌉ +
4 lines at the job's N, at most 16 rows. The grant's words take 80 bytes past
the counter on its line; the senders' line is 16 × 8 bytes. The watch in
`in_rounds` runs from row 0's counter to the end of the reduce-scatter grant
counter: it covers the credit counter, both grant counters and every row counter
waited on, and no put lands inside it. `number_taken` zeroes the rows + 3
counters, the grant's words and the senders' line. A team takes the tree when it
has more than 65,536 nodes, or when ⌈log₂ N⌉ exceeds the ceiling + 1; the grid
pins the boundary both ways: 31 nodes at a ceiling of 4 take Träff's, 64 do not.
