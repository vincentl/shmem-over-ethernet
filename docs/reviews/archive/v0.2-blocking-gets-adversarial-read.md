## Verdict

**Nothing fatal.** The pipelined path is the nonblocking get's own, and it holds:
- a place's block is past the pool, never claimed by a context, and named by no other context's
  token, scan or promise;
- every reader of the pool goes through `osoe_node_context`, at the new stride, and nothing in the
  tree indexes it by `contexts_per_pe` alone;
- the lead lands the pieces as it lands any nonblocking get's, returns their credits, and refuses a
  reply whose record is not in flight with that very token;
- a quiet on the default context, serial or shared, neither waits for nor misses a blocking get's
  pieces, nor the get theirs;
- a place reused after its thread exits starts with equal counters;
- a get toward a node-mate never comes here, and a progress helper fails by name as on `main`;
- the record's tables match the evidence cell by cell, and the internode test's moved count is right.

**Three findings are rated *should fix*:**
1. **`shmem.stat` fails, in every preset and both builds**: the library writes layout 37 and osoe-stat
   decodes the watch word only to 36, so it prints it raw - "encoding unknown" - and the test's check
   of the decode fails at 2 PEs and more.
2. **A stalled blocking get of more than a piece names neither its node nor its PE**, and osoe-stat
   calls it nonblocking gets: the phase-1 diagnosability review's D-6, undone for every get the change
   touches. Its test pins it with a get of 8 bytes.
3. **No test has several threads' multi-piece blocking gets in flight at once**, the cross-thread
   copy into a blocking caller's `dst` that the change brings among them.

Eight notes follow.

**1. `shmem.stat` fails, and osoe-stat stops decoding the watch word: the library writes layout 37,
and osoe-stat decodes to 36** (*observed in source*; not run)

- **Where:**
  - `shmem/src/node.h:34-36`: `OSOE_NODE_LAYOUT 37`;

- **Fix:** `OSOE_SEGMENT_NEWEST_LAYOUT` 37, the list's line - "37 each PE's pool of context blocks

**11. A thread that leaves in the middle of a get** (*observed in source*; outside what OpenSHMEM
defines)

- A thread cannot leave inside a call except by cancellation, `siglongjmp` or `pthread_exit` from a
  handler, which `base/sweep.h` puts outside what the library supports.
- If one is cancelled in the new wait - `count_if_lost`'s `nanosleep` (`:493`) is a cancellation
  point - its pieces stay in flight:
  - another thread's `make_room` later copies them into the dead thread's `dst`, its stack perhaps;
  - the place's block owes their bytes to the next thread on the place, whose wait covers them only
    once some `make_room` has copied them out;
  - `own_gets.pending` leaks.
- On `main`, a cancelled blocking get left one piece's extents held, and wrote nowhere.
- Nothing to fix while cancellation is unsupported. The record's "nothing outlives a thread that
  exits" holds for threads that leave by returning, and could say so.

## What is sound

- **Routing.** A get toward a node-mate never reaches `remote_get`.
  - `osoe_transport_get` applies it in the calling thread when `osoe_routes_in_process` holds
    (`transport-am.c:347-351`), under `OSOE_FORCE_REMOTE` too, which denies the pointer and keeps the
    route.
  - A job of one node never enters `transport-internode.c`.
- **A place's block is its thread's alone.**
  - Program contexts claim blocks 0 to `contexts_per_pe - 1` (`transport-am.c:140, 148, 271`), so the
    block `contexts_per_pe + place` is never claimed, never live, and named by no context's token,
    scan or promise.
  - Every reader of the pool goes through `osoe_node_context`, whose stride is now
    `contexts_per_pe + threads_per_pe` (`node.h:1254-1258`): `transport-am.c`,
    `transport-context.h`, `copy_out` (`:783-785`), `landed()` (`progress-host.c:171`). A `git grep`
    of `contexts_per_pe`, `contexts_offset` and `osoe_node_context` across `shmem`, `tools` and the
    tests finds no other reader; osoe-stat does not read the pool.
  - The lead lays the section out at that stride (`node.c:786-788`), and a PE attaches only with the
    lead's `OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS` and `OSOE_MAX_THREADS` (`:1213-1227`), so both read one stride.
  - The guard `own <= OSOE_LANDING_OWNER_MASK` and `landed()`'s widened bound agree: no token can name
    a block the pool lacks.
- **Tokens and the lead.**
  - A multi-piece blocking get's tokens carry the nonblocking bit, so the lead lands them through
    `landed()` and returns their credits itself (`progress.c:654-675`), unchanged.
  - `landing_request` still refuses a reply whose record is not ISSUED with that very token
    (`progress-host.c:125-143`).
  - The two kinds cannot be confused: a slot's token has the bit clear and names a place, below
    `threads_per_pe`; a piece's has it set and names a block at or past `contexts_per_pe`.
- **A quiet beside it.**
  - The serial wait walks its own pending list (`:1108-1113`); the shared one scans for
    `owner == c->block` (`:1177-1179`); OSOE_CHECK_QUIET's promise check filters the same way
    (`:1723-1725`). None of them waits for, or copies, a blocking get's pieces.
  - A blocking get's wait never sees another context's pieces: its pending list is its own, its
    counters its place's.
  - The one crossing - an issue that finds the zone full and copies out any landed piece (`make_room`,
    `:832`) - credits the piece's own block and wakes its waiter, as for nonblocking gets.
- **Completion.**
  - The thread is its block's only issuer and the call is synchronous, so `issued`, loaded as the wait
    begins (`:1100`), is final.
  - `copy_out` adds to `bytes_copied` after its `memcpy` (`:779-789`), so `copied == issued`, loaded
    seq_cst, means every byte is in `dst` and visible to the caller, whichever thread copied it.
