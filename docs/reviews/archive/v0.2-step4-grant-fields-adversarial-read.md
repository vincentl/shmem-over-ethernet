## Verdict

**Nothing fatal.** The change is right where it matters:
- **The layout:** every offset is inside its number's words at every row count from 1 to 16, and
  the region reserves exactly what the code writes.
- **One writer:** every field has exactly one writer, and the check names that writer.
- **The handover** is ordered.
- **The next collective:** a field is read and cleared before its writer can write it again.
- **The check:** a correct program cannot fail it.
- **The model:** the claim that it needed no change is true.

**Four findings are rated *should fix*:**
1. the one test that runs the check does not pin it, so the verification's mutant passes that

2. three README numbers the change moved and left (a fourth, `17.0 GiB`, sits at the rounding
   edge);
3. the README's ThreadSanitizer sentence on the head, which the lead is to rewrite;

## What is sound

**The layout** (*computed*: `layout.c`, with `internal-region.h`'s own functions).

The number's words take R + 3 + G lines, where G is `ceil((8 + 24R) / 128)`. Each row of the
table is in bytes from the number's first line:

| Rows | Grant lines | Counter | Last field ends | Senders' line | Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 1 | 896 | 1,024 (line end) | 1,024-1,152 | 1,152 |
| 6 | 2 | 1,024 | 1,176 | 1,280-1,408 | 1,408 |
| 10 | 2 | 1,536 | 1,784 | 1,792-1,920 | 1,920 |
| 11 | 3 | 1,664 | 1,936 | 2,048-2,176 | 2,176 |
| 15 | 3 | 2,176 | 2,544 | 2,560-2,688 | 2,688 |
| 16 | 4 | 2,304 | 2,696 | 2,816-2,944 | 2,944 |

- **Every row from 1 to 16:** the last field ends inside the grant lines, and the senders' line
  is the number's last.
- **The zeroing at a number's take** stays inside:
  - lines 0 to R + 2 by atomic stores;
  - R × 24 bytes from the counter + 8;
  - the senders' line.
- **The round wait's range is exact.** It runs from arrival counter 0 to the grant counter + 8,
  and field 0 begins at that byte. The watch's grain is 4 bytes (`watch-range.h`), so a field's
  put never matches it.
- **The fields are 8-aligned,** which keeps two helpers' landings in different ThreadSanitizer
  granules. Fields 0 to 4 share the counter's line, and fields 10 and 15 straddle a line. Neither
  matters, since nothing watches a field and none is atomic.
- **The region and the code agree.** `circulant_line`'s stride, `osoe_internal_scan_offset`, the
  lanes' offset and `node.c`'s region size all go through `osoe_internal_team_circulant_bytes`.
- **The record's line counts are right:** one line to 32 nodes, two from 33, three from 1,025,
  four past 32,768.
- **A team's rows never exceed the layout's.** `osoe_circulant_rows(n)` is ceil(log2 n) for n
  from 2 to 70,000, the layout's rows are that capped at 16, and `shape_of` (`:760-761`) lets a
  team take Träff's only where its rows fit. The entry and the reduce both call `shape_of`, so
  they agree.
- **Tested at runtime:** `shmem.circulant.33`, `.64` and `.70` run the second grant line and the
  moved senders' line, with the reuse and few-elements phases. All three passed in the
  verification's debug and release runs at none, two and three helpers.

**One writer per field.**
- **The schedule:** a round's skip s_k is a function of N and k alone
  (`osoe_circulant_round`), with 1 ≤ s_k ≤ N - 1, and the skips are strictly decreasing.
- **So field k at lead i has one writer:** receiver j writes field k at j - s_k, so field k at
  i is written only by i + s_k. That is never i itself, and it is exactly `round.to` of i's own
  round k, the lead the message names.
- **Checked by program:** `rows.c` confirms it for every lead and round from 2 to 300 leads,
  with `collective-circulant.c` itself.

**The handover.**
- **One receiver's put and add:** a receiver's 24-byte put and its fenced add go on one
  (context, PE). `transport.h`'s fenced flag places the add after the put, and one owner applies
  both in order.
- **The adds are release operations:** each add is a `seq_cst` `fetch_add` (`rmw.h`), so every
  add heads a release sequence that holds every later add to the counter.
- **The sender reads after an acquire:** its load in `osoe_region_wait` is acquire, and reads a
  value at least base + rows. That load therefore synchronizes with every receiver's add, and
  every field's bytes are visible to it.
- **Helpers make no difference here.** Different receivers' puts land on different threads, into
  different bytes.
- **The counter cannot count ahead into the next collective** (next paragraph), so the value read
  is this collective's.

**The next collective.**
- **Every writer waits on the reader.** The writer of field k at i, i + s_k, is the lead to which
  i adds an allgather grant at the end of its reduce-scatter round k (`:1739-1742`).
- **And the reader acts only after reading:** i sends that add after `take_round_grants` has
  read and cleared every field.
- **So no writer can return before the clear.** Every lead waits for all of its allgather grants
  before its first allgather round (`:1696-1700`). A writer therefore cannot return from this
  collective, and so cannot grant the next one, before i has read and cleared. That holds even
  where a round moves no pieces.
- **A stale field cannot pass:**
  - a cleared one is epoch 0, and epochs start at 1;
  - one from the team that held the number before was zeroed at the take.
- **A team with fewer rows than the layout's:** its fields past them are zero from the take,
  never written and never read.

**The check.**
- **Every mismatch ends the job by name:** the call, or a lane that is 0 or past any PE's.
- **A correct program cannot fail it.** Both sides compute every term from their own call
  (`collectives.c:185`, `:471`):
  - the identity by `reduction_identity(operation, element)`;
  - the bytes as count × width;
  - the epoch as the team's k + 1.
- **In place changes none of them.**
- **Collect's lengths reduction** is the same sum of `uint64_t` over the same member count on
  every lead.
- **The lane** is `own_lane() + 1`, from 1 to 1,024.
- **The message** names the right receiver (its node and PE), the round in the headers'
  numbering, and both calls, with the format's arguments in order.

**The model.** `circulant_model.py` gave each grant `RS_FIELD(frm, k)` tagged with its
collective and checked every field (`fields`, `:460-467`) before this change. The claim holds,
with N3's two differences.
