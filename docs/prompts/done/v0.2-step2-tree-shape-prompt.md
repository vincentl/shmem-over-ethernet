# v0.2 step 2: the tree's shape from the link - fan-out and window chosen per collective

**Status: written on 2026-10-05 from the author's decisions for v0.2
(`docs/decisions/2026-10-05-v0.2.md`, items 1 and 7), the Pi cluster's
measurements of 2026-09-26 (`docs/pi-cluster-results.md`, finding F2, and
design §10's fan-out item), and the code as the 0.1.0 tag left it. It is the
second step of `docs/prompts/task-v0.2.md`. It follows step 1 but depends on
nothing step 1 builds; it precedes step 3, Rabenseifner's reduction, which
reuses the chooser this step builds.**

What this step changes, in one sentence: the internode tree a large reduction
or broadcast runs on stops being one fixed shape for every size and becomes
the shape the link makes cheapest for that call - a fan-out and a window
chosen by one deterministic rule from three numbers measured at start-up, the
same on every node - so that an 8-byte reduction keeps the star that is twice
as fast on the Pi and a 1 MiB one takes the binary tree that is 1.5 times
faster there, with nobody setting anything.

The author's rule for v0.2 (item 7): decisions go by performance and
elegance, not by what they cost in code or tests. So this brief asks for the
measured form - design §10 called it "the next step, once a real fabric exists
to measure" - and not for a fixed threshold.

It is written for a session that starts cold, in this repository, with the
VMs and the Pi cluster below. Nothing in it needs the conversation that wrote
it.

The author's goals, the axes to judge the work by:

- **Internode algorithms logarithmic in node count**, in time and in memory.
- **Intranode algorithms linear or better in PEs per node.**
- **A path to RoCE at ~10⁶ PEs that no interface blocks** - a constraint on
  the seams, not work here. The chooser is arithmetic on the node map and
  three numbers; a backend that has its own collectives ignores it.
- **For this step:** no setting a user must tune to get the fast shape;
  every claim of speed timed before it merges (the rule of 2026-09-26).

## Part 1: the cost model and the link

### The three numbers

A shape's cost on a link is set by three numbers, every one of them already
in the Pi's records:

- **h**, the hop: the one-way latency of a small message between two leads,
  through the sender's progress thread and the receiver's. On the Pi about
  85 µs (half the 169-175 µs round trip of an 8-byte put and quiet); on
  loopback in the VM tens of microseconds.
- **o**, the message cost: what the progress thread spends per message it
  seals or applies, which is what makes a parent of 32 children slower to
  release than a parent of 4 at 8 bytes. On the Pi a few microseconds
  (`osoe-pacer mailbox`'s 250,000 messages a second is one thread's rate at
  16 PEs; the per-message term is of that order).
- **B**, the bandwidth: the bytes a lead moves through its link a second.
  111 MiB/s on the Pi's gigabit; gigabytes a second on loopback.

### The cost of a shape

For a tree of fan-out k over the team's N nodes, a message of n bytes in
pieces of s = `OSOE_INTERNAL_PIECE_BYTES` (16 KiB) with a window of W pieces
in flight, the Pi's table is reproduced by one rule: **the time is the
busiest link's bytes over B, plus a hop and a message cost per child at every
level, plus the window's fill where the pipeline is shallower than the tree.**

- The busiest link is read off the tree itself, which the chooser builds
  (`osoe_collective_tree` is cheap): a node with c children receives c
  vectors and sends one in a reduction's up phase and the reverse in its
  release, and receives one and sends c in a broadcast, and the pipelined loop
  overlaps the up and the down phases piece by piece, so a reduction costs its
  busiest node max(in, out) × n / B and a broadcast c_max × n / B. The Pi:
  a 1 MiB reduction on four nodes at k = 4 is the root's 3 vectors in, 28.2 ms
  at 111 MiB/s, measured 28.2; at k = 2 it is 2, 18.8, measured 19.4; the
  broadcasts 27.2 and 18.2 measured against 28.2 and 18.8. Note the four-node
  cluster hides the interior node's cost: in a tree deeper than one level an
  interior node of a reduction sends its partial up *and* k results down, so
  its link carries (k + 1) n, and the model must count that even though the
  Pi cannot show it - the VM's logical nodes show the shape, and the timing
  simulation the time.
- Every level costs a hop h plus k × o for the children a parent serves
  before it can release; L = ⌈log_k N⌉ levels. The Pi: k = 4 is one level on
  four nodes and k = 2 is two, and k = 4 is twice as fast up to 1 KiB.
- The window's fill: with W pieces in flight and a piece costing s / B to
  move, a level whose W × s / B is shorter than its hop waits; the Pi's D = 1
  lines against D ≥ 2, and D = 2 against D = 4 and 8 at k = 2, are this term.

Write the rule as a function of (N, n, s, k, W, h, o, B) in one place -
`tools/modelcheck/shape_timing.py` first, in `grant_timing.py`'s form, then
the same arithmetic in C - and fit h, o and B to the Pi's table: the fit
must land every line of 2026-09-26's broadcasts and reductions within the
run-to-run noise of those files, k = 2, 3 and 4, D = 1 to 8, 64 KiB to 1 MiB,
and the f2 sweeps from 4 bytes to 64 KiB. If a term is missing, the table
says so; add it and say why in the record. Do not fit more constants than the
three numbers mean: a fourth constant is a hypothesis, named as one.

### The chooser

`osoe_shape_choose(N, n, link, ceiling, window_capacity)` returns the (k, W)
of least cost with k from 2 to the ceiling and W from 1 to the capacity, ties
to the smaller k and the smaller W; a barrier (n = 0) and a gather have no
shape to choose, and a message in the small class (`osoe_collective_class`)
keeps its team's small slots and the ceiling's tree as today - the small path
is one hop in one piece and its tree is the record's. **Deterministic on the
same inputs on every node**: integer arithmetic only, in nanoseconds and
bytes, no floating point, so two nodes never round apart. Pure, with no
reference to `osoe.bootstrap` or any global, so a test calls it with any
inputs. Its cost in time is a few hundred integer operations a call, nothing
against a hop.

### Measuring the link

At start-up, once the leads' mesh is built and before the start-up release
travels down (`rendezvous.c`, `send_down`), **the root measures its link
against its first child**: a burst of small messages for h and o (a round trip
alone gives h; a burst of m round trips in flight gives the per-message cost
as the slope), and a stream of pieces for B, a few hundred kilobytes, long
enough that the slope is the link's and not the hop's. Medians, not means.
Then the three numbers travel down in the down header with `node_count` and
`run_count` - a new `DOWN_MAGIC`, since the header's shape changes - so
**every node holds the root's numbers and computes the same shape**. A job of
one node measures nothing and chooses nothing.

What it costs: one round trip burst and one stream at start-up, a few
milliseconds on the Pi and under a hundred on a 100 Mbit link; record it in
the start-up time the README states. If the measurement is noisy enough to
matter, the choice is coarse - a factor of two in n moves a crossover one k -
so the shape tolerates it; the record says what the Pi's start-up measures
run to run.

Overrides for determinism, three settings read at start-up and agreed as
`OSOE_COLLECTIVE_FANOUT` is: `OSOE_LINK_HOP_NS`, `OSOE_LINK_MESSAGE_NS` and
`OSOE_LINK_BYTES_PER_SECOND`. Set, they replace the measurement; the tests
use them to pin a shape; the README's row says they exist for tests and for a
user who knows their fabric, and that the default is the measurement. All
three or none: one set alone is refused by name.

### The settings

- **`OSOE_COLLECTIVE_FANOUT` becomes the ceiling**: the largest fan-out a
  shape may take and the fan-out the layout is sized for - the small slots'
  rows, the words, the lanes' rows, the reply pool's degree, the scan area.
  Range 2 to 32 as today. **Default 8**, since on hundreds of nodes a star of
  8 is three levels where 4 is five, and the chooser keeps 8-byte reductions
  at the ceiling when o is small against h: the Pi's numbers decide whether 8
  is right there, and the record says what the default costs a PE in memory
  against today's 4 (the small slots are (k + 1) rows a team number, 256
  numbers at the default; the lanes (k + 1) × D × 16 KiB a thread, address
  space untouched until used). If the measurements say 4, the default is 4.
- **`OSOE_COLLECTIVE_PIPELINE` becomes the window's capacity**, the depth
  the lanes are sized for; the chooser picks W up to it. **Default 4**, since
  k = 2 at D = 2 was slower than the default on the Pi's 1 MiB reduction and
  D = 4 and 8 were 1.5 times faster. Range 1 to 8 as today.
- **`OSOE_COLLECTIVE_SHAPE`**: `link`, the default, chooses; `fixed` pins
  every shape at the ceiling and the capacity, which is the tag's behaviour
  and the A side of every measurement. Agreed at the rendezvous like the
  others.
- The three link overrides above.

Every one checked and agreed three ways - read, among a node's PEs, between
nodes - as `OSOE_SMALL_COLLECTIVE_BYTES` is, with a new `UP_MAGIC` for the
larger agreement. The README's settings table takes the new rows and the
changed meanings in the same commit.

## Part 2: the shape on the data path

### Reductions and broadcasts

- `find_lead_tree` already builds a broadcast's tree per call; make it build
  from a shape - `osoe_shape_choose` on (N, n) - and make the reduction's
  tree the same per call instead of `reduction_tree` built once. Cache the
  trees a team has used by k (at most ceiling − 1 of them) so a call costs
  the chooser and a lookup, not a build.
- The loop's window becomes the shape's W, not the capacity.
- **Child positions and the counters.** `arrival_counter(number, position)`
  is cumulative per child position and compared at least against
  `arrival_base[c]`; positions are at most the ceiling, so every shape fits.
  A position unused in one call does not advance, and a child's position in
  its parent's rows is its position in *this call's* tree; the grant already
  carries epoch, identity, bytes and lane, and gains nothing, since both sides
  compute the shape before the grant. State in the loop's header comment the
  rule that makes this safe: a team's collectives are not concurrent (§9.10),
  so every member computes the same shape for the same call, and the counters'
  cumulative bases are per position whatever child held it.
- **Memory.** Nothing new: the layout is the ceiling's. Check that
  `osoe_internode_generic_number_taken`'s zeroing covers every position up to
  the ceiling, as it does today.
- The barrier keeps the record's tree at the ceiling: a barrier has no bytes
  and the fewest levels win.

### Scans

The scan's rank tree (`scan_place`, `scan_children`) takes k the same way,
from a shape chosen on (members, n): every member scans at its team rank's
place in a pre-order k-ary tree (design A), so the shape is of the ranks, not
the nodes, and N is the member count. If the scan's pieces sized to the scan
(step E part 2) make the window term differ, the model says so first.

### Measurements

On the Pi at 4 × 4 and 4 × 1, `OSOE_COLLECTIVE_SHAPE=link` against `fixed`
at the tag's defaults (k = 4, D = 2), alternating, the e-band probe's sizes
from 8 bytes to 1 MiB for reduce, broadcast and scan, with `osoe-bench` and
the OSU three (`osu_oshm_reduce`, `_broadcast`, `_fcollect`):

- the claim: no line slower than `fixed` by more than the noise, the 1 MiB
  broadcast about 1.5 times faster and the reduction 1.2 to 1.45, as the
  k = 2, D = 4 to 8 runs of 2026-09-26 were, and the lines below 1 KiB
  unchanged;
- the band between 1 KiB and 64 KiB, where the crossover lies: the chosen
  shape no worse than the better of k = 2 and k = 4 at every size, within the
  noise;
- the start-up time with the measurement against without (`fixed` skips it).

In the VM, 16 and 64 logical nodes at `link` with the loopback link's numbers
and with the Pi's numbers forced through the overrides: the shapes chosen
(exported through a counter per k, `collectives.shape.k`, in the metrics) and
the tests green at both.

Record every number in `docs/pi-cluster-results.md` as a new session, with

## Stop and ask

- The fit failing to reproduce any line of the Pi's table within its noise
  without a fourth constant: put the term and its meaning to the author
  before building on it.
- A shape that two nodes could compute differently from the same numbers -
  any floating point, any dependence on a node's own state.
- The start-up measurement costing more than 50 ms on the Pi, or varying run
  to run by more than a factor of two in any of the three numbers.
- The default ceiling of 8 or capacity of 4 costing a PE more than twice the
  tag's memory for small slots and lanes at the defaults: put the numbers to
  the author with the alternatives.
- Any line on the Pi slower under `link` than under `fixed` by more than the
  noise.
