## 10. Step 8 slice 3, lock-free issue: built, timed

§4.11 items 1, 2 and 4 over slice 2's structures. **Decision 5, taken here:**

**The first build hung**, and the design had it right: the wait re-read the
slot's highest segment on every check, and another thread's put raised it
past the number the acknowledgement was asked through. It now waits for the
number it asked for, then clears the bit and looks again, as
`dirty_set_model.py` has it.

**The timing nearly stopped the slice.** In the VM, four threads showed no
gain and I recorded the brief's stop condition in the work-in-progress
commit. The Pis, with four cores for the PE's threads, show the claim: four
threads went from 0.89 of one thread's rate to about 1.4. The VM's
four-thread run was oversubscribed. What bounds more than one thread's rate
beyond that is below the context - the connection buffer or the progress
thread - and is recorded in design §4.11 as the next limit.

**Mutations, and the stop.** Nine of the brief's mutations were run, each
shown in its build, the thread, context, get, lock and quiet tests three
times each. Five are caught: the bit cleared without the re-check (on one
map of three), the copier crediting pair 0, a lock taken on issue, the quiet
not copying out its own pieces, and a piggyback on `full == e`. **Four
escape**: the issuer never reloading the generation (`noretag`), the bit set
before the raise (`bitfirst`), the raise by a plain store (`plainmax`), and a
`pe_quiet` storing `full`. Each is a narrow interleaving that
`get_completion_model.py`, `dirty_set_model.py` and `pe_quiet_model.py` fail

still in flight. The brief makes an escape a stop: **not merged; the
author's question** is whether the models stand as these four's evidence, or

## 12. The four escaping mutations: experiments and a recommendation

**Why they escaped.** Each breaks the protocol only in a narrow interleaving,
and even then the harm shows only if a reply or an acknowledgement is still
on its way when the quiet returns. A thread that reads its own put back rides
the same ordered connection as the put, so it can never see one in flight;
and a quiet's scan copies out any landed piece of its context whatever its
pair, which hides a mis-tagged get whose reply has already landed.

**Two test hooks** (`transport-am.c`, off unless set, one flag load a point):
`OSOE_CHECK_QUIET=1` makes every wait on a shared context check its promise
at return - every node it covers acknowledged through what writes that had
returned before the call had put there, and no nonblocking get of the
context whose call returned before the call still in the landing zone - and
end the job naming the break; `OSOE_TEST_PERTURB=1` yields or sleeps up to

## 13. OpenSHMEM 1.6's last two gaps, closed (the author asked, 2026-09-26)

- **The profiling names of the seven unprefixed deprecated routines.** Both

- **Partial active sets** (step 5 slice 7, brief
  `docs/prompts/phase1-step5-slice7-prompt.md`). Member-only algorithms over
  the program's `pSync`, taken here without a stop: counted signals, a
  dissemination barrier, binomial trees for broadcast, fcollect, collect and
  reductions, direct puts for alltoall. The whole job keeps its team path.
  The old whole-job test's check that a partial set ends the job now checks a
  set past the job. Undo: the dispatch in each routine back to the whole-job
  check.

**The review** (`docs/reviews/archive/phase1-step5-slice7-review.md`) found
no blocker: an interleaving model of `active-set.c` ran 20,000 random
