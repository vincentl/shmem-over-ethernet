**What is built** (the brief's decisions 1-6, made concrete):

- `OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS` (default 64, 1 to 4,096) read in `bootstrap.c`, agreed
  between a node's PEs in `node.c`, in `SHMEM_INFO`'s text.
- Layout 21: each local PE's pool of `struct osoe_context_block` (a cache
  line: a state word claimed by compare-and-swap, `bytes_issued`,
  `bytes_landed`, their wake word) after every other section of the control
  segment, four directory fields appended (232 bytes; `tools/stat/segment.h`
  mirrors it, `OSOE_SEGMENT_NEWEST_LAYOUT` 21). The landing slot loses the
  counters. Block 0 is the default context's.
- **A nonblocking get's token names its context's block in bits 48-59**
  (`OSOE_LANDING_CONTEXT_SHIFT`), the sequence in bits 16-47: the lead
  credits that block in `landed` (progress-host.c) and the wire is
  unchanged. *Undo*: a `context` field in the landing record, read by the
  lead before it clears the token.
- `transport.h`: `osoe_transport_context_create(options)` (NULL when the
  pool is empty or memory is short) and `_destroy` (implicit wait), option
  flags SERIALIZED, PRIVATE, NOSTORE; NOSTORE skips the fence in fence and
  wait. The get and wait contracts now say a *context's* gets complete
  together.
- `transport-am.c`: a process array of `OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS` transport
  contexts, element i on block i; the in-flight records are per context (a
  list through each record's `next`) over one free list and **one bump
  arena, given back whole once no context has a get in flight**; an issue
  that finds no record or no room completes its own context's gets first,
  then every other context's until there is room (`am.landing_full` counts
  it, as before). *Undo*: fixed arena slots per record (arena / records
  each), which slice 2's extents will make anyway.
- New API file `context.c`/`context.h` (in `OSOE_API` of
  `CheckBoundary.cmake`): a pool of `OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS` elements whose
  addresses are the handles, `osoe_ctx_of`, `osoe_ctx_resolve` (translates
  the PE through the context's team, fatal by name outside it) and
  `osoe_ctx_resolve_list` (pe_quiet's targets, **team-relative too**, by
  §9.5's blanket rule; *undo*: world ranks in `quiet.c`); the four routines;
  a PRIVATE context's creator recorded, a destroy from another thread fatal.
  `team.c` lost its stubs and `osoe_ctx_of`.
- **A team's destroy destroys the shareable contexts created from it**
  (before its sync) **and ends the job by name on a live PRIVATE one**
  (§9.4.8 says undefined); **the last finalize destroys every context left,
  private or not, silently**, before its barrier (`osoe_context_finalize`).
- `rma.c`, `amo.c`, `signal.c` (put_signal resolves once for both halves),
  `quiet.c`, `session.c` use the resolution. Counter `am.gets_completed`
  (pieces copied out by a wait on their context).
- `test-context.c` and its ctest entries after the get-nbi map tests in
  `shmem/tests/CMakeLists.txt`: exhaustion at max - 1 and reuse, the
  options, destroy's implicit quiet, two contexts' quiets independent
  (pinned by `am.gets_completed`), the landing zone shared between contexts
  (pinned by `am.landing_full`), a team context's numbering by value with
  put, AMO, put_signal, pe_quiet, get and get_nbi, a shareable context left
  for the team's destroy and a private one left for finalize; seven misuses
  by name; `OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS` disagreeing, invalid, and a pool of 3.
  `test-team.c` and `test-team-split.c` now expect creation to succeed.

**The failure that was not a defect.** The session ended believing that
early completion of another context's gets copied two of four replies out
wrong, at `OSOE_GETS_IN_FLIGHT=4` on the multi-node maps. The next session
(Opus 5.5) read it again: the test expected the right neighbour's table words
1 and 2 to hold their first values, and the test's own exhaustion and option

**The adversarial review** (a read-only Opus agent over `8bbc15a..a3b960a`)
found one blocker, four should-fix and seven nits; all are fixed on the
branch but N3 and N6, which are stated in design §4.11 instead:

- **B1, NOSTORE skipped the only fence that orders intranode puts.** The
  brief's decision 6 had NOSTORE skip the serializing fence in fence and
  quiet; `base/order.h` says those fences are what order a put's `memcpy`
  on the intranode path, so a NOSTORE context's own puts lost their order
  (on aarch64, a flag seen before its data). *Decided without the author*:
  NOSTORE is recorded and changes nothing, which conforms (the spec permits,
  never requires, the skip); design §4.11 corrected. *Undo*: skip the fences
  only on a context that has issued no intranode put or get since its last
  fence or wait, a per-context flag set on the in-process branches. No test
  can show B1 on the VM's x86-64; a Pi could.
- **S1-S4, the test**: finalize's implicit destroy is now pinned by a get in
  flight on a leftover private context, whose word must be filled when
  `shmem_finalize` returns (and nothing puts into a freed heap any more);
  team contexts on `SHMEM_TEAM_SHARED` (a table on `0,1,2,0,1,0`) and on a
  stride of -1 are checked by value; the arena running out across contexts
  (one word in flight on A, a get on B as large as the arena) is pinned by
  `am.landing_full` and `am.gets_completed`; pools of 1 and 3 run.
- **Nits**: an out-of-pool block in `landed` is fatal rather than a silent
  hang; the token's 32-bit sequence and `am.gets_completed`'s early copies
  are said where they are defined; stale comments in `shmem.h` and
  `rma-typed.c`; `transport.h` no longer describes AM's pool.
- **N3 and N6, stated rather than changed**: contexts share the landing
  arena, so one context's traffic can complete another's gets early (slice
  2's extents remove it); team destroy ends the job on a live private
  context where finalize destroys it silently, both undefined by the spec.

## 5. The scans and E, built (phase 1 step 10 slice 1)

Branch `phase1-step10-slice1`. E first (`977b3b3`): the record carries
`(members, world_start, world_stride, strided)`, layout 22, and the upcall is
gone. Then the scans as the brief's design A: `osoe_internode_scan` called by
every member of a team spanning nodes, its generic implementation in
`internode-generic.c`; one-node teams scanned by the local lead in
`collectives.c`; typed routines and C11 generics for every type SUM takes;
the scans' area at the internal region's end, per team number since the
review (below).

Taken without the author, each with its undo:

- **A scan on a team spanning nodes takes no epoch** (brief decision 6 said
  it would): every member runs the tree itself, and taking an epoch would
  mean the node's arrival counters, an intranode barrier for nothing. So
  osoe-stat's published epoch does not count such scans. *Undo*: arrive and
  release on the team's counters around the call, as a reduction does.
- **Pieces go one at a time**, each up and down before the next (brief
  decision 4 said so): a scan of more than 16 KiB of elements costs
  2 ceil(log_k M) steps a piece. *Undo*: pipelining with D down-slots and
  up-slots, which the model would need to cover first.

**The first build's verification** (`67e3207`, `build/scratch/scans/v1/`):
debug 451, release 473, asan 450, tsan 353; tier 3, 21 of 21; six mutations
caught of six (no READY, one READY for every team, counts reset, the left sums
dropped, the down without the member's own source, the one-node lead's
inclusive and exclusive swapped); the load loop; SOS's three `shmemx_` scan
programs, renamed, on 0,1, 0,0,1,1 and 0,1,2,0,1,0; the sweep at 151.

**The review** (a read-only Opus agent over `e3cc949..67e3207`), no blocker in
the tree as it stood, and fixed:

- **The shared slots would break `THREAD_MULTIPLE`** (should-fix): two
  threads of a PE may scan two teams at once (spec §9.4), and the PE's one set
  of slots and its process-wide counts would race. *Decided without the
  author*: the slots and counters are the team number's, on every member's
  region, 4 KiB slots, 1.3 MiB of address space a PE at the defaults; READY is
  gone, since consecutive scans on one team are safe by the tree. The model
  gains the `per_team` design and its breakage (`per_team_pe_counts`, stuck).
  *Undo*: the shared area and READY, which the model still carries, if
  address space matters more than threads.
- The one-node lead now works a 16 KiB chunk at a time, not the whole scan
  (it had allocated twice the scan in private memory); the internode path's
  buffers are a piece, not a slot, each; a hung scan's blocked record names
  the PE awaited; an unpublished record says so; `find_intervals` lost the
  old loop's scaffolding; the seam's call lost the scan count it no longer
  needs.
- The test gains a map of one PE a node (0,1,2,3), where the three teams of
  two span nodes, and every other type SUM takes once.

**Verified at `98c5306`** (`build/scratch/scans/v1/`): debug 452, release 474,

## 9. Step 8 slice 2, threads: built, reviewed, fixed

Built on `phase1-step8-slice2` to the brief (`docs/prompts/done/phase1-step8-slice2-prompt.md`).
Its new test, `test-multiple.c`, found two bugs before the review:

- **A remote AMO without a fetch never released its context's mutex**, so
  under `MULTIPLE` the next call on the context hung. Below `MULTIPLE` no
  mutex is taken, which is why nothing else saw it.
- **The test raced between its waves**: a fast PE's next wave put into a slow
  neighbour's rows while it still checked them. A barrier between waves.

The adversarial review (`docs/reviews/archive/phase1-step8-slice2-review.md`)
found three blockers and four should-fix items; all are fixed:

- **B1, the thread table.** `OSOE_MAX_THREADS` now counts application threads
  only, **default 16**, at most 1,023; the agent's block is one more. The
  design said the node's CPU count; 16 is taken instead because a four-core Pi
  would get 4 and threads oversubscribe cores freely. Undo: the default in
  `metrics.c`.
- **S1, memory orders.** The room-making wait's checks and the landing's state
  store are `seq_cst`, as the wait word's contract requires.
- **S2, tests that could not see the slice.** The threaded test now runs under
  tsan on one PE a node, where every writer of a PE's memory is a thread of one
  process; each worker has four nonblocking gets in flight, so the zone
  overfills (pinned by the room-making count); workers also get on the default
  context and take locks.
- **S3 and S4, thread exit.** A released block is forgotten by its thread, and
  the give-back shares a mutex with finalize's release of the pool.
- **S5, the blocked record's mutex** is taken only under `MULTIPLE`, as the
  brief's decision 1 requires.
- **S6, the notes.** Design §3.14, §4.6 and §4.11 carry the slice as built.

**Taken without the author:** `shmem_init` stays at `SERIALIZED` where the
brief said `MULTIPLE`, so a program that never asks for threads pays nothing
for them; `shmem_init_thread(SHMEM_THREAD_MULTIPLE)` gives `MULTIPLE`. Undo:
`OSOE_INIT_LEVEL` in `init.c`. And an `OSOE_GET_LANDING_BYTES` that is not a
whole number of 1 KiB extents is refused by name instead of rounded down.

**Not fixed, as nits:** a thread's token sequence restarts when a block is
reused (a thread never exits with a reply in flight, so no reply can match a
stale token); `complete_gets` rescans its pending list on each wake (slice 3
replaces the list); a second thread of one PE taking a lock its PE holds ends
the job with a message about the PE, not the thread; the context pool's scans
are unlocked reads of words claimed by compare-and-swap; a set
`OSOE_GETS_IN_FLIGHT` is ignored, and the README no longer lists it.

**Verified** at `fa59c93`: the four presets, 460, 482, 459 and 359 tests;
tier 3, 21 of 21; seven mutations, each shown in its build and each caught
(the non-fetching AMO keeping the mutex, the extent claim by plain store, no
copy-out while making room, the copier crediting the default context, every
blocking reply in thread 0's slot, the agent counted in `OSOE_MAX_THREADS`,
an unaligned landing size accepted); the SOS sweep, 159 programs passing on
both maps, the eight threaded ones among them, and the same four known
failures as before. The load loop failed the new zone-overfill pin on the
two multi-node maps: under 16 busy loops a PE's threads ran one at a time,
and four pieces each never overfilled 8 extents. The pin, not the library:
each thread now has 12 pieces in flight, more than the zone alone, and the
three maps then passed 30 runs each under the same load, and the threaded
tests in every preset. That test-only change is the one difference between
the verified tree and the merge.
