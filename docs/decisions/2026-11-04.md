# 2026-11-04: the cluster tests merged, their five questions decided

## The ask

The author, reading where the tree stood before the first large-cluster
session: the harness branch `claude/adoring-cohen-777120` - the `cluster`
preset, tier 3's programs across real nodes under `srun` through `ctest`,
built on 2026-11-02 at his answer 4 of [2026-11-01](2026-11-01.md) - was

cluster agent to that preset at gate A4. "Merge the harness", to the question

The record [2026-11-02-cluster-tests](2026-11-02-cluster-tests.md) put the
five as choices without a recommendation line; the recommendations below are
this record's, and the author took them with the merge. Each can be undone on

## The five

1. **The uneven and interleaved maps stay, through `srun-map.sh`'s
   hostfile.** The interleaved map `0,1,2,0,1,0` is the one most of tier 3's
   list runs on; striking the hostfile maps would leave 14 of the 37 programs
   and lose the map the detector and the deadlock tests are registered on.
   The script's premise - that Slurm numbers a step's nodes in
   `scontrol show hostnames`' order under `--distribution=arbitrary` - is
   what `cluster.map.0-1-2-0-1-0` and `cluster.map.1-1-0-0-2-2` check first,
   on the Pi when it is back or at the cluster's gate A4; a wrong premise
   fails those two tests before any program runs on the map.
2. **`OSOE_CLUSTER_NODES` is empty by default.** A site without the setting
   registers every map, and `srun` refuses the ones it cannot give, which
   `ctest` reports as failures a reader can see; a default of four would be
   the Pi's number pretending to be every site's. The runbook and the
   playbook's A4 set it explicitly.
3. **No `global-exit` test of the step's status under `cluster`.** `srun -K0`
   with the step's status against the caller's is a lesser check than
   `global-exit.sh`'s three, and a test under the same name would be read as
   the same check; the runbook's §7 by hand stands, as the README's bullet
   says.
4. **Every cluster test is `RUN_SERIAL`.** Two steps on the same nodes would
   share their cores and `/dev/shm`; `ctest -j` on the cluster preset now
   runs them one at a time whatever it is given, and the preset's own
   invocation is unchanged.
5. **Nothing to do.** The playbook's three amendments reached `main` twice by
   two routes that agreed; the merge confirmed it.

## The eight questions left, answered

The author, the same afternoon, asked for the open questions with a
recommendation each and took them all: "Take them all". The five are the
huge-pages proposal's (`docs/design/huge-pages-proposal.md`, "Questions for
the author"), the three the small-collective model's record's
(`2026-11-02-small-collective-model.md`, "For the author").

1. **`OSOE_AM_HUGEPAGES` is removed now**, not held for the cluster's first
   rung: a no-op since 2026-10-11, and the study found that only the
   `/dev/shm` mount option governs, which no rung changes. Removed from
   `am-configuration.c` and `.h`, `connection-area.c` (the `MAP_HUGETLB` flag
   and its retry; the mapping is one `mmap` of ordinary pages, its comment
   saying why), `init.c`'s `SHMEM_INFO` text, `unknown-settings.c`'s list,
   the invalid-setting test's case, the README's row and the design notes'
   table. The text in its place: the README's "Memory" says the library
   asks for no huge pages, what `huge=within_size` on `/dev/shm` gives, its
   2 MiB rounding and the study's measurements; the ceilings' `/dev/shm` row
   names the rounding; the design notes' §3.4, §6.2 and the leftovers'
   paragraph say the same in their words.
2. **The site is asked for `huge=within_size` only after a random-access
   rung shows the gap**, not as a matter of course: a mount is the whole
   node's, and the ladder's random-access line at 64 PEs a node is the
   before-and-after that justifies the ask. A0 records `/dev/shm`'s mount
   options among the site's facts; C14 carries the gate.
3. **No proposal of its own for `memfd` heaps now**: it waits for a
   workload the mount option is refused for, or page tables measured in
   gigabytes on a lead. C14 says so.
4. **The NUMA setting is written into C14 and built on the first two-socket
   node**, not blind: nothing here has two sockets.
5. **The measurement patch stays out of `main`**, which the merge of the
   morning did; the answer confirms it.
6. **The small class is not shaped from the link before the top rungs time
   `OSOE_COLLECTIVE_FANOUT=16` against 8**: nothing to gain below 585 nodes,
   one level at 4,096. If 16 wins there by the model's margin, the rule is
   a few lines. The §5 row's "Decided by" cell carries it.
7. **The message cost's two readings are a question for the ladder**: the
   barrier read beside the start-up's message cost in one job on the
   cluster, which says whether the data path's fan is measuring P38's idle
   wakes. The same cell.
8. **No dissemination barrier**, unless the ladder's barrier sits far above
   the model's line and the receiver's cost table does not explain it. The
   barrier row's cell.

## How to undo

Each of the five on its own: strike the hostfile maps by the harness record's
"To undo"; give `OSOE_CLUSTER_NODES` a default in `shmem/tests/CMakeLists.txt`;
add a `cluster.global-exit` test; remove the `RUN_SERIAL` line from
`osoe_cluster_register` and the README's clause. The merge itself is a merge
commit, reverted as one.
