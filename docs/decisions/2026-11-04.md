# 2026-11-04: the cluster tests merged, their five questions decided

## The ask

The author, reading where the tree stood before the first large-cluster
session: the harness branch `claude/adoring-cohen-777120` - the `cluster`
preset, tier 3's programs across real nodes under `srun` through `ctest`,
built on 2026-11-02 at his answer 4 of [2026-11-01](2026-11-01.md) - was
the one thing blocking the session, since the playbook on `main` sends the
cluster agent to that preset at gate A4. "Merge the harness", to the question
whether its five questions go by their recommendations.

The record [2026-11-02-cluster-tests](2026-11-02-cluster-tests.md) put the
five as choices without a recommendation line; the recommendations below are
this record's, and the author took them with the merge. Each can be undone on
its own.

## What was done

  through `a3e4bc89` (the record's question 5), and the two sides agreed.

- The index's line under "How work is done" carries the five.

## The five

1. **The uneven and interleaved maps stay, through `srun-map.sh`'s
   hostfile.** The interleaved map `0,1,2,0,1,0` is the one most of tier 3's
   list runs on; striking the hostfile maps would leave 14 of the 37 programs
   and lose the map the detector and the deadlock tests are registered on.
   The script's premise - that Slurm numbers a step's nodes in
   `scontrol show hostnames`' order under `--distribution=arbitrary` - is
   what `cluster.map.0-1-2-0-1-0` and `cluster.map.1-1-0-0-2-2` check first,
   on the Pi when it is back or at the cluster's gate A4; a wrong premise
   fails those two tests before any program runs on the map.
2. **`OSOE_CLUSTER_NODES` is empty by default.** A site without the setting
   registers every map, and `srun` refuses the ones it cannot give, which
   `ctest` reports as failures a reader can see; a default of four would be
   the Pi's number pretending to be every site's. The runbook and the
   playbook's A4 set it explicitly.
3. **No `global-exit` test of the step's status under `cluster`.** `srun -K0`
   with the step's status against the caller's is a lesser check than
   `global-exit.sh`'s three, and a test under the same name would be read as
   the same check; the runbook's §7 by hand stands, as the README's bullet
   says.
4. **Every cluster test is `RUN_SERIAL`.** Two steps on the same nodes would
   share their cores and `/dev/shm`; `ctest -j` on the cluster preset now
   runs them one at a time whatever it is given, and the preset's own
   invocation is unchanged.
5. **Nothing to do.** The playbook's three amendments reached `main` twice by
   two routes that agreed; the merge confirmed it.

## How to undo

Each of the five on its own: strike the hostfile maps by the harness record's
"To undo"; give `OSOE_CLUSTER_NODES` a default in `shmem/tests/CMakeLists.txt`;
add a `cluster.global-exit` test; remove the `RUN_SERIAL` line from
`osoe_cluster_register` and the README's clause. The merge itself is a merge
commit, reverted as one.
