## Batch 1: D30 and D23

**D30, a library line split between PEs.** glibc 2.37 and later write an unbuffered stream's
formatted output through 128 bytes at a time, so the library's 22 `fprintf(stderr, ...)` lines
longer than that reached stderr in pieces another PE's output could split (round nine's P9-7).
`osoe_print_line` (`base/print-line.h`) formats the whole text - into 1 KiB on the stack, into
allocated memory beyond - and writes it with one `write(2)`, keeping `errno`; every one of the 22
goes through it, its words unchanged. `base.print-line`: four processes print 2,000 lines of 200
bytes and one of 3,000 each to one pipe, and the reader finds all 8,004 whole; the same program
printing by `fprintf` broke 7,142 of them on the VM's glibc 2.41. `shmem.whole-lines`
(`cmake/CheckWholeLines.cmake`) fails on a `fprintf` to stderr in the library's sources, comments
included as `CheckMemoryOrders.cmake` reads them: at `main` it named the twelve files, and the new
header's own comment once. The kit's static step and the static expression gain it.

**D23, a launcher timeout that said nothing of the job.** The timeout line names the job -
"timeout after N s; killing K process(es) of job J" - by its id, the launcher's process id that
`osoe-stat` takes; before ending the job the launcher runs `osoe-stat --summary J` from beside
itself (the same directory as installed, or the build tree's `tools/stat`), its output on stderr,
ten seconds at most, reaped by its pid so that the ranks' `waitpid(-1)` finds only ranks; a
forwarded SIGINT or SIGTERM names the job too. The line's first form ("job J, K process(es) still
running, ended now") broke `refusal.sh`, which reads the count from "killing K process": twelve
refusal tests failed in every preset until the words came back. `shmem.launcher.timeout-summary`;
`--help` and the README say it.

Verified in the `osoe` VM: release, debug, asan, tsan, tier 3 and the single-node build without a
warning; the static checks 17 of 17; release and debug 954 of 954, asan 936 of 936, the whole tsan
preset 652 of 652 with the gate passing, the single-node suite 435 of 435 - the refusal tests
after the timeout line's words were restored, 73 of 73 in release, debug and asan, 72 in tsan
and 44 in the single-node build. No process killed for memory. 102 items.
