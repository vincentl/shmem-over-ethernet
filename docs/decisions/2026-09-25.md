# Decisions of 2026-09-25

The author's answers to the status page's open questions, after the Pi
cluster's first session (in chat, 20:45): "approve 1, 2 (c) and 3 - go".

1. **Team creation, as proposed** (`docs/prompts/phase1-step7-slice1-prompt.md`,
   decision 16 of 2026-09-24): `OSOE_MAX_TEAMS` a runtime value, default 32,
   agreed at start-up; a node block, an internal-region block and a record per
   team number; numbers claimed from one counter on world PE 0 and never
   reused. The storage slice first, which changes no behaviour, then creation.
2. **Large-message collectives: both fixes, pipelining first.** Several 16 KiB
   pieces in flight per level of the internode tree, which speeds broadcast,
   reduce and fcollect alike and keeps the depth at log N; then an allgather at
   the internode seam for fcollect in log N steps (design §5.8; decision 8 of
   2026-09-24).
3. **`SHMEM_THREAD_MULTIPLE`'s open points** (design §10, "points §4.11 leaves
   open"): a full thread table ends the job with a diagnostic naming
   `OSOE_MAX_THREADS`; concurrent blocking gets claim fixed landing-zone
   extents by compare-and-swap over a bitmap; a landed nonblocking get has one
   copier, claimed by compare-and-swap on its record's state. The last two are
   model-checked before any code.
