**T28, a model of `active-set.c`** (round three's review, K6). `active_set_model.py`, from one
to ten members through up to seven calls back to back on one pSync: the barrier and sync,
broadcast from any root, fcollect, collect with counts of zero among them, reduce and alltoall,
and an application's puts before a `shmem_barrier`; the network a thread that lands puts and
atomic adds in any order a fence, a quiet or a put-with-signal allows, members on one node
applying at once. Its properties: every result, no member leaving a barrier before all have
entered, a write landing after its target entered, a read before its target returned, pSync
restored, no hang. The review's six mutations and thirteen more each fail; 98 cases, 3.4
million states, about two minutes on the Mac. A Sonnet agent the builder asked to audit it
found the model's programs, 1,664 of them generated, the same as its own reading of the C.
What it found doubtful, no correct design failing:

- `arrive`'s quiet orders nothing the model can see, the signal's own read-modify-write being
  the release; it waits for the member's earlier puts at every arrival, a latency's cost.
- The counts, the `>=` and the add of the negative matter to the dissemination barrier alone;
  on the trees' words no add for a later call arrives early.
- Breaking the fence or the signal's order breaks the transport's contract only; both backends
  place in issue order, so neither can fail today.
- OFFSET and GRAND are written by a put and read by atomics, TOTAL set by an atomic and read by
  a get; nothing in `transport.h` makes a put and an atomic on one word exclusive, so they are
  safe through the release's and the arrival's order alone, which the model's atomic words
  cannot question.
