# Decisions in force

One line a decision that still holds, grouped by what it governs. Each names
who took it and links the dated record that explains why and says how to undo
it. The dated files beside this one are the history; this page is what to read
to know the rules today. A decision that changes one of these lines updates it
here in the same commit, and a reversed one moves to the last section.

"Author" means the project's author decided; "Claude" means a session decided
without the author, under a standing mandate, and the record carries the undo.

## Scope and goals

- **Release point first.** 1.5 complete through step 8, 1.6 less its known gaps, the SOS suite clean of its own extensions, a green Pi runbook, a license, `cmake --install`, a README, a version tag. Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 2.
- **RoCE is a constraint, not work.** The seams stay RDMA-capable; no RoCE backend before the release; Soft-RoCE at most after it. Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 1.
- **Step 8 in three slices.** Contexts serial; then threads behind an interim per-context mutex; then a lock-free shared mode. Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 3.
- **Scans modeled before built, right after step 8 slice 1.** Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 6.
- **Scan design A.** Every member scans at its team rank's place in a pre-order k-ary tree, on its own thread. Author, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 4.
- **collect's lengths through the reduction tree.** Per-member lengths travel through the tree's fixed per-team slots into private memory, never a symmetric heap borrow. Author, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 6.
- **Team creation as proposed.** A runtime `OSOE_MAX_TEAMS`, per-team storage blocks. Author, [2026-09-25](2026-09-25.md) item 1. *Its team numbers from one counter, never reused, are reversed (below).*
- **Team numbers are each member PE's resource, given back at destroy; a team keeps counters and small slots, a PE lends the large ones.** The two steps of `docs/design/team-resources-proposal.md`, both before the first tag; a significant rewrite accepted for the better design. Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 2.
- **The release fixes before the tag** are the plan's (`docs/prompts/done/phase1-release-fixes-plan.md` §2) as the brief `docs/prompts/done/task-release-fixes.md` carries them: D1 and D3 the author's, D2 confirmed, D4 to D9 as recommended. Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) items 1, 3 and 7.
- **Large collectives get both fixes.** A pipelined internode tree, then a dedicated internode allgather. Author, [2026-09-25](2026-09-25.md) item 2.
- **Small gathers keep Bruck's cost.** No two-slot staging and no crossover. Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 5.
- **No strided primitive at the transport boundary.** `iput` batches puts; `iget` uses the nonblocking get. Author, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md), "Typed RMA".
- **Deprecated active-set collectives run on any active set**: the world team's path for the whole job, member-only algorithms over `pSync` for any other. Author, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 13.
- **The deprecated routines' profiling names come in both readings**, `pstart_pes` (Sandia's) and `pshmem_start_pes` (literal). Author, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 13.

## Protocols and data structures

- **Progress.** An agent thread in every PE; the local lead's agent alone drives the transport; no helper process. Design §3.12.
- **Atomics.** ISO C11 `stdatomic.h` with named orders only; no intrinsics, no assembly, no spin before blocking. Design §3.16.
- **Blocking.** One watch slot per local PE with a 32-bit futex sequence word. Design §4.9.
- **Collective epochs.** Cumulative 32-bit counters with equality waits; sense reversal retired. Design §5.4.
- **The internode seam.** A blocking call on the lead's thread carrying the operation and element, with a generic tree any backend may reuse; a backend's only routing input is the node map. Design §4.10, §5.8.
- **The agent's boundary call.** `osoe_transport_agent_wait` and `agent_exit` give a backend the agent thread and a last drain. Design §3.12, §4.10.
- **Introspection.** `osoe-stat` reads the control segment through `/proc/<pid>/fd`; the segment's name is removed at init. Design §3.14.
- **Job handshake.** An optional `OSOE_JOB_SECRET`, proved by a SipHash-2-4 challenge, never sent; a caller's claims are judged only once it has proved it, and the listener serves sixteen callers at once. Design §6.2; the proof first, [2026-10-02-release-fixes](2026-10-02-release-fixes.md) Part 3.
- **Start-up is loud.** Every start-up wait says what it waits for after 30 s, one line a node, and ends nothing; every PE checks every setting that shapes the node or the job before its node joins; the lead makes its segment before anything can refuse, and a refusal before the segment tells the node (a tombstone from a lead that has none); a launch is told from another by the same names (`SLURM_RESTART_COUNT`, `OSOE_LAUNCH`). Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 3 (D2); built in [2026-10-02-release-fixes](2026-10-02-release-fixes.md) Part 3. A node-mate takes the job tables and the connection area only from the lead whose control segment it joined, by the creator's pid. Claude, [2026-10-04-review-fixes](2026-10-04-review-fixes.md) item 1.
- **Abort.** An abortive goodbye on every open connection, not a flood down the tree. Claude, confirmed by the author, [2026-09-23-autonomous](2026-09-23-autonomous.md) item 12.
- **The abort word.** One 64-bit word claimed by one CAS; the lead's agent re-wakes every local agent. Claude, [2026-09-23-autonomous](2026-09-23-autonomous.md) items 13-14.
- **Global exit.** The PE its node's word names - the caller whose declaration won - runs `exit(status)` and leaves last, after every other PE of the job; a caller that lost runs `exit(0)`; every other PE flushes and calls `_exit(0)`. So a launcher ending the step at the first bad exit has nobody left to kill, and the job's status is the caller's; the one residual is two callers on two nodes at once. Claude, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) item 10; the status, Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 1; the caller last, Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 9; built in [2026-10-02-release-fixes](2026-10-02-release-fixes.md) Part 2.
- **A reset connection** is judged only once the peer's goodbye and end of stream have both arrived. Claude, model-checked, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md), "Question 10".
- **PE deaths** are found by pidfds each agent polls in its own sweep. Claude, confirmed by the author, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) items 11-13.
- **No development watchdog.** The ending agent names the routine its PE was blocked in, in every build. Claude, confirmed by the author, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) items 11-13.
- **Slurm's variables** are read only when `OSOE_PROCID` is unset, never mixed with osoe's. Claude, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) item 4.
- **A host whose name resolves to loopback** binds and advertises the address it would use to reach a peer. Claude, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) item 8.
- **Typed RMA covers every Table 5 type.** Diagnostics name the routine actually called and its element width. Claude, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) item 16.
- **fcollect's lead gathers its own members' blocks;** a peer's buffer is written only after that peer has entered. Claude, [2026-09-24-day](2026-09-24-day.md) items 7-8.
- **The distributed lock** is an MCS queue lock homed by address hash, model-checked first-come first-served. Claude, [2026-09-24-day](2026-09-24-day.md) item 4.
- **Symmetric statics** are gathered by a linker script over the whole executable; `-no-pie` is required and `-static` refused. Claude, [2026-09-24-day](2026-09-24-day.md) item 5.
- **The pipelined internode tree** lets a lead take whichever ready input comes first. Claude, [2026-09-26-overnight](2026-09-26-overnight.md) item 1.
- **Internode allgather** runs Bruck's steps among node leads at the seam. Claude, [2026-09-26-overnight](2026-09-26-overnight.md) item 5.
- **A team's record carries its world ranks** as start, stride and member count; the upcall that computed them is gone. Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 7; built in [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 5.
- **Contexts.** A pool of `OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS`; a nonblocking get's token names its context; `NOSTORE` is recorded and still runs the ordering fences. Claude, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 2.
- **Shared contexts issue without a lock**: a generation word and two counter pairs for gets, an atomic dirty bitmap with compare-and-swap highest segments for puts, and a mutex taken only by waits; a wait that finds a node behind after its clear re-sets the bit and moves on. Author (the design and the change to item 2), [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) items 10-11.
- **`OSOE_CHECK_QUIET` and `OSOE_TEST_PERTURB`** are test hooks kept in the library, off by default, and the threaded test runs with both in every preset: together they catch the race mutations the tests otherwise cannot see. Claude, recommended to the author, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 12.
- **Team destroy** tears down its shareable contexts and is fatal, by name, on a live private one; finalize destroys what is left. Claude, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 2.
- **A node's team blocks are a pool found by name**, the local id of the team's local lead there and its number, through a map; claimed from the top of the pool down; the progress engine, the internode seam and the arrival scan know a team by its block, and a barrier message names it by its name on the node it goes to. The team redesign's step D, part 1, from the author's decision; built by Claude, [2026-10-03-team-storage](2026-10-03-team-storage.md), with its choices' undos.
- **A team number is each member PE's own**, agreed by the split - one AND reduction over the parent of every member's free mask, the lowest bit common to all - and given back at destroy; a node's block goes back to its pool with the last of the team's members there to leave, its generation raised, and the engine, the generic phase and a handle tell one team under a block or a number from the next by that generation. The team redesign's step D, part 2, from the author's decision; built by Claude, [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md), with its choices' undos.
- **A team's local lead clears its name's map entry as it leaves**, not the block's last holder: no message for the team reaches the node once a member there has left. Claude, [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md), modelled in `team_block_model.py`.
- **A split under `SHMEM_THREAD_MULTIPLE` takes its numbers pending between two reductions**, when any PE of its parent has that level: a word per number set by compare-and-swap, a split waiting for a word held by one of a higher parent number and losing its round to one of a lower, and a second reduction saying whether every member took them. In place of the proposal's mask token, which deadlocks a valid program. Claude, [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md) (D7 as redesigned), modelled in `split_numbers_model.py`.
- **A pool found empty makes a split's round busy, not dry, while a block may be coming back**: in the split's first round, and while a block has fewer holders than it was named with, a count its next claim zeroes; otherwise the split fails on every parent PE, naming `OSOE_TEAM_BLOCKS` and the node. A destroyed team's place is promised to a later split only on a node where the split's would-be lead there was in the team, or the team's members there are all in the split's parent. Claude, [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md), modelled in `pool_dry_model.py`.
- **A team's local release waits until its RELEASEs have left the control queues**, so that the reply pool's term, degree × `OSOE_TEAM_BLOCKS`, bounds a block's forwards when it is given back and claimed again (design §3.8). Claude, [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md).
- **Cross-node scans** use slots and counters per team number, never shared per-PE slots, so threads cannot race on them. Claude, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 5.
- **Cross-node scans keep D pieces in flight**, the reductions' `OSOE_COLLECTIVE_PIPELINE`, by five rules the scan model checks: the window, copy before count, a counter per child position, down only after up, and D pieces' totals and source kept. Author ("pipeline it"), [2026-09-28-overnight](2026-09-28-overnight.md) item 1.
- **A program's own deadlock ends the job once it is proved**: each PE counts its threads asleep in library waits, counted at the sleep and never across a post; a node's lead samples in two halves with a pass of its engine between; the root runs two collections `OSOE_DEADLOCK_SECONDS` apart, every node quiet and unmoved. A thread the library cannot see, or a stopped process, is never judged deadlocked. Author ("build it"), built by Claude, [2026-09-28-overnight](2026-09-28-overnight.md) item 2.
- **A vanished node with data in flight is found within keepalive's budget**: once a keepalive interval the lead reads `TCP_INFO`, and a peer whose kernel has acknowledged nothing for the whole budget while segments wait ends the job, naming it; a stopped peer's kernel still acknowledges, so it is waited for. Claude, found by tier 3, [2026-09-28-overnight](2026-09-28-overnight.md) item 4.
- **Every agent asks for a short scheduler slice, and may run on any CPU of its node's tasks where asked** (`OSOE_AGENT_CPUS=node`; the default, `task`, keeps it on its PE's CPUs) - the union of the bindings the PEs publish; the PEs' own threads keep theirs, and nothing of the system's configuration changes. Author (the question) and Claude, [2026-09-28-overnight](2026-09-28-overnight.md) item 3.

## Settings and defaults

- **`OSOE_MAX_TEAMS`** defaults to 64, at most 1,024. Author, [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md) item 4. It counts the numbers one PE holds at once, the three predefined included, since step D's second part ([2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md)). *Defaults to 256 with step E; `OSOE_SMALL_COLLECTIVE_BYTES` (the largest collective that moves through a team's own slots, default 1 KiB, agreed at the rendezvous) with step E. Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 8 - decided, built on the team slices.*
- **`OSOE_TEAM_BLOCKS`**, a node's pool of team blocks: default 1,024, from 8 to 65,536, agreed among a node's PEs and free to differ between nodes; a node holds that many teams at once, a destroyed team's block given back by the last of its members there to leave. Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 8; built by Claude, [2026-10-03-team-storage](2026-10-03-team-storage.md) and [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md).
- **`OSOE_MAX_CONTEXTS`** defaults to 64, from 1 to 4,096, agreed by a node's PEs at start. Claude, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 2.
- **`OSOE_MAX_THREADS`** counts application threads, default 16, at most 1,023; the agent's block is one more, and a thread past it ends the job by name. Claude, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 9.
- **`shmem_init` gives `SERIALIZED`**; `shmem_init_thread` gives `MULTIPLE` when asked. Claude, [2026-09-26-evening](2026-09-26-evening.md) item 9.
- **`OSOE_COLLECTIVE_PIPELINE`** defaults to 2, from 1 to 8, agreed at rendezvous. [2026-09-26-overnight](2026-09-26-overnight.md) item 1.
- **`OSOE_DEADLOCK_SECONDS`** defaults to 10, at most 86,400; 0 turns detection off. Claude, [2026-09-28-overnight](2026-09-28-overnight.md) item 2.
- **`OSOE_AGENT_SLICE_US`** defaults to 100 µs, from 100 to 100,000, or 0 for the kernel's own; **`OSOE_AGENT_CPUS`** defaults to `task`, each agent on its PE's CPUs, and `node` lets it run on any CPU of its node's tasks. Claude, [2026-09-28-overnight](2026-09-28-overnight.md) item 3. The slice stays 100, with both sides of its trade in the README's row: the author read the plan's recommendation on 2026-10-02 and struck nothing ([2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 7, D5).
- **Keepalive** is 60 s idle, 10 s between probes, 6 probes; `TCP_USER_TIMEOUT` is not set; `OSOE_KEEPALIVE` overrides. Claude, [2026-09-23-autonomous](2026-09-23-autonomous.md) item 17.
- **`OSOE_HEAP_BASE`** must be page-aligned, and an unaligned one is refused by name. Claude, [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) item 17.
- **The `/dev/shm` capacity check** refuses by default; `OSOE_SHM_CAPACITY_CHECK=0` makes it a report. Design §6.2.
- **Dependencies.** The distribution's liburing 2.6 or later, never a bundled copy; CMake 3.25 or later. Design §1. Python 3 is optional: the model checker and the stress tester's counting build use it, and where CMake does not find it they are left out. Claude, on the plan's recommendation (`docs/prompts/done/phase1-release-fixes-plan.md` §4.5), built in [2026-10-02-release-fixes](2026-10-02-release-fixes.md) Part 1 item 6.
- **Names.** The library is `libosoe` with hidden visibility; `OSOE_`/`osoe_` are internal, `SHMEM_` belongs to the specification. Design §1.

## How work is done

- **Merge as each slice verifies**, and keep going without waiting, with every decision written down with its undo. The author's standing direction, [2026-09-23-autonomous](2026-09-23-autonomous.md); reaffirmed as "merge into `main` at green checkpoints" for the release fixes and the team slices, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 4.
- **The README is true of the tree at every commit.** A change carries the README sentences for what it changes; a sentence later work will replace says so ("temporary", "work in progress"). Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 6.
- **Verification before merge**, on the exact tree that merges: four presets, tier 3, mutations on a copy, the load loop, the SOS sweep, and an adversarial review whose findings are fixed first. [2026-09-24-day](2026-09-24-day.md).
- **`shmem.floor`'s pins are checked only when asked.** Its three pins are the project's regression guard, set on its own machines: they are checked in an optimized build with `OSOE_TEST_FLOOR_PINS=1` in the environment, which the project's own verification sets by configuring with `-DOSOE_TEST_PINS=ON` (tier 3's floor tests included); a stranger's `cmake --workflow` prints the ratios and checks none. `env.echo-uring`'s pins are not behind the switch. Author (accepted as the plan recommended), [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 7; built in [2026-10-02-release-fixes](2026-10-02-release-fixes.md) Part 1 item 5.
- **A speed claim needs a timing simulation** beside its model. [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md).
- **A subagent's report is evidence, not a verdict.** [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md).
- **Model-checker scripts run once, in the release preset.** Claude, [2026-09-23-autonomous](2026-09-23-autonomous.md) item 9. They carry the ctest label `model`, which the `release` test preset leaves out - they are most of an hour - and the `models` test preset runs alone; the README's first command is the suite without them. Author, [2026-10-02](2026-10-02.md) item 7 (D7); built in [2026-10-02-release-fixes](2026-10-02-release-fixes.md) Part 1 item 7.
- **The costly THREAD_MULTIPLE models are opt-in** with `OSOE_MULTIPLE_MODELS=ON`. Claude, [2026-09-23-autonomous](2026-09-23-autonomous.md) item 10.
- **The VM is shared.** Long runs take the lock, measurements never overlap another session's, and runs are killed by PID. [2026-09-26-day](2026-09-26-day.md).
- **Slurm stays installed in the VM,** and fixtures come from real Slurm and the Pi cluster. [2026-09-24-overnight](2026-09-24-overnight.md) items 5-6.
- **Disk.** Merged worktrees are removed; uncommitted work is archived first. Author, [2026-09-26-overnight](2026-09-26-overnight.md) item 7.

## Tools

- **A stress tester, `osoe-pacer`**, in `tools/pacer/`, built in four phases (`docs/design/osoe-pacer-proposal.md`); every exercise verifies; not installed until phase 4 decides. Author and Claude, [2026-09-26-night](2026-09-26-night.md) items 1 and 4.
- **`parallel_print` stays in the tool**, written against the OpenSHMEM API alone; `shmemx.h` stays empty. Author, [2026-09-26-night](2026-09-26-night.md) item 3.
- **`parallel_print`'s labels** are world PE numbers in Slurm's bracket syntax, with the team's name before the bracket on any team but the world. Author, [2026-09-26-night](2026-09-26-night.md) item 2.
- **The pacer's memory budget** defaults to half the heap; `--memory` takes a size or a share, refused with the `SHMEM_SYMMETRIC_SIZE` to set when the heap cannot hold it. Author, [2026-09-26-night](2026-09-26-night.md) item 5.
- **The pacer's briefs are `task-stress-phaseN.md`.** Author, [2026-09-26-night](2026-09-26-night.md) item 7.
- **Every check of the pacer has a plant**, and a plant's test requires each line its checks print, in any order (`cmake/PacerPlant.cmake`). Author (the rule) and Claude (the script), [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) items 1-2.
- **The sort's oracle is count, sum, xor and a hash sum**: the first three are blind to a byte swapped between two keys. Claude, [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) item 3.
- **A pacer exercise fits itself to the budget**, which bounds its symmetric and its private memory each; `step` is told which step is the last. Claude, [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) item 4.
- **`parallel_print` syncs at least once a call**, and a program calling it on two teams in turn syncs the second team between them. Claude, [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) item 6.
- **A plant a collective could absorb is forced**: each kind's wrong input is one its result cannot hide (past every input for max, below for min, 0 for and, all ones for or), or PE 1's own expectation where its input may not reach the result. Claude, [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) item 11.
- **Across nodes the pacer times round trips, never one-way trips**: clocks differ between nodes, so one-way times are taken between node-mates only, and `mailbox` times a checked ping-pong between the PEs of one rank on paired nodes. Brief and Claude, [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) item 13.
- **The pacer's `teams` renews a strided team every third round**, one PE holding at most six numbers - the four strided teams and a whole-team `split_2d`'s two - and `--teams-exhaust` splits once more after its destroys, to show the numbers came back. Claude, [2026-10-03-team-allocation](2026-10-03-team-allocation.md), Tests.
- **The pacer's `threads` tests run the library's two test hooks in every preset**, with a landing zone its gets overfill (`OSOE_GET_LANDING_BYTES=8192`), on five maps and on 0,1 under tsan. Claude, [2026-09-27](2026-09-27.md) item 15.
- **The pacer calls every typed routine by both spellings**, the C11 generic and the type's own name, since a generic cannot reach a routine whose type is another's typedef; `rma` flips a case's spelling every second pass, so four passes run both on both contexts. Claude, [2026-09-27-afternoon](2026-09-27-afternoon.md) items 2 and 8.
- **The pacer checks what moves as bytes and what is computed as values**: RMA every byte of the region, gaps and canary included; `p`, `g`, atomics and collectives by oracles that do not depend on order; symmetry as offsets from an anchor, never as addresses. Claude, [2026-09-27-afternoon](2026-09-27-afternoon.md) items 2-5.
- **Every collective the pacer times or checks follows a sync of its team**, so a dest is ready on every PE before any calls (§9.10). Claude, [2026-09-27-afternoon](2026-09-27-afternoon.md) item 5.
- **`mix` moves a set-up exercise between teams by one hook, `rebind`**: `setup` allocates for the world and starts on its team, `rebind` moves it onto another afresh; an exercise whose cases cycle carries its rounds across periods. Claude, [2026-09-27-evening](2026-09-27-evening.md) items 1-2; kept when the author asked whether to go to the brief's four, [2026-09-27-night](2026-09-27-night.md) item 9.
- **A team exercise calls nothing of the whole job's** in its steps: `mix` runs it on a team while other teams run others, and a world collective there would be matched by the others' next one. Claude, [2026-09-27-evening](2026-09-27-evening.md) item 2.
- **`waits` is one of `mix`'s six team exercises**, and `mix-cycle` runs 288 periods on four teams: long enough for `collectives` to reach every case on a team, and enough teams that a world collective called on one cannot be cancelled by the next team's before a world period. Author and Claude, [2026-09-27-night](2026-09-27-night.md) "What the answers changed" and Verification.
- **The pacer's smoke tests run in every preset.** Author, [2026-09-27-night](2026-09-27-night.md) "What the answers changed".
- **`osoe-pacer` is installed** beside `oshcc` and `osoe-stat`, finding the library by its runpath from its own directory: `osoe-pacer --quick` under a cluster's launcher is how a user checks osoe works there. Author, [2026-09-27-night](2026-09-27-night.md) "What the answers changed".
- **The sort's local LSD sort takes 11-bit digits**, six passes, the last of the 9 bits left. Author, [2026-09-27-night](2026-09-27-night.md) item 7.
