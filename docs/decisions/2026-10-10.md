# 2026-10-10: the author's answers after v0.2 step 4's merge point

On the morning of 2026-10-10 the author answered the review's three questions
(`docs/decisions/2026-10-10-step4-merge-point.md`, "The author's answers to the
synthesis's questions"):

- 0.2 is not tagged until it is known whether more hardware can be had;

- keepalive on one machine was put to them with the mechanism and the fix.

In the afternoon, two answers ("accept 1 & 2"), and a question of what else to
do before the hardware comes.

1. **Keepalive is off by default on one machine's loopback.**
   - **The rule:** where every node of a job has a loopback address - one
     machine's logical nodes, which `osoe-launch` starts - and `OSOE_KEEPALIVE`
     is not set, a node's lead sets no TCP keepalive on its node-pair
     connections, and the silent-peer check, which shares its budget, is off
     with it. A job across machines keeps both, as does tier 3's, whose
     namespaced nodes have addresses of their own; `OSOE_KEEPALIVE` set holds
     everywhere.
   - **Why:** on one machine the kernel is one. It closes a dead logical node's
     connections itself, and answers for a stopped one, so a probe there can
     only be lost, and an overloaded machine's loopback backlog loses them:
     healthy jobs of tens of logical nodes ended as vanished hosts (the review,
     correctness Q2-1).
   - **Built** in the bootstrap's default, where the lead opens its wires
     (`progress-host.c`), and recommended there rather than in `osoe-launch`.
   - *Undo:* the default back to 60,10,6 on every job, and the README's
     sentence for `osoe-launch` back.
2. **A blocking get issues its pieces as a nonblocking get does,** before Part
   4's measurements, models first (`docs/design/blocking-get-proposal.md`, the
   recommendation, (a)).
   - **What it buys:** a large get about a fifth faster on the Pi's gigabit, and
     from a mebibyte over a fast path from 6 to 10 times a put's time to about
     three.
   - **Not built at this record.**
   - **The landing zone's size,** which holds a nonblocking get there, is put
     to Part 4 (the proposal's (d)).
