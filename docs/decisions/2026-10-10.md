1. **Keepalive is off by default on one machine's loopback.**
   - **The rule:** where every node of a job has a loopback address - one
     machine's logical nodes, which `osoe-launch` starts - and `OSOE_KEEPALIVE`
     is not set, a node's lead sets no TCP keepalive on its node-pair
     connections, and the silent-peer check, which shares its budget, is off
     with it. A job across machines keeps both, as does tier 3's, whose
     namespaced nodes have addresses of their own; `OSOE_KEEPALIVE` set holds
     everywhere.
   - **Why:** loopback addresses put a job in one network namespace, with no
     link between its nodes to cut, and its kernel closes a dead logical node's
     connections itself and answers for a stopped one, so a probe there can
     find nothing. It can only be lost, and an overloaded machine's receive
     backlog loses them: healthy jobs of tens of logical nodes ended as
     vanished hosts (the review, correctness Q2-1). Not "one kernel": tier 3's
     namespaces share one kernel too, and a cut link there loses its probes
     meaningfully.
   - **Built** in the bootstrap's default, where the lead opens its wires
     (`progress-host.c`), and recommended there rather than in `osoe-launch`.
     The lead says its choice under `SHMEM_DEBUG`, from the silent-peer
     check's own start.
   - **Tests:** `shmem.keepalive.loopback-off.0-1` (`OSOE_KEEPALIVE` unset,
     both leads' sockets off, the silent-peer check's line for "on" failing
     it), `shmem.keepalive.set-on.0-1` (set, so on), and tier 3's
     `tier3.pi.keepalive.default-on.0-1` (unset, between addresses of their
     own, so on, and the check's line for "off" failing it). The tier-3 test
     came from the read below: before it, a default that turned keepalive off
     on every job passed the suite.
   - **Not lost on loopback:** a killed logical node's kernel closes its
     connections, and a stopped one's still answers. What is lost is the
     loopback failing under a running job - `lo` taken down, or a firewall
     dropping 127/8 - which ended such a job in two minutes and now waits for
     the launcher's timeout or `OSOE_NO_PROGRESS_TIMEOUT`.
   - **Read** adversarially by a fresh agent at `e2bb1e9`
     (`docs/reviews/archive/v0.2-loopback-keepalive-adversarial-read.md`):
     nothing fatal; its two should-fix findings - the default between
     machines pinned by no test, and sentences the change made false or left
     short - and its notes 3, 5, 7 and 8 were taken.
   - **Verified** in the development VM

       and two, ten minutes of mix among it;
     - at `9b6ba72` the final check failed both keepalive tests in every
