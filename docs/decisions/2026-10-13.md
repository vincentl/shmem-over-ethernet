## B11, the kit's switch to a site's binding

`docs/fast-link/binding.sh NODES site|none`, run on the controller after `slurm-setup.sh`:
`site` writes `TaskPlugin=task/affinity,task/cgroup`, `ProctrackType=proctrack/cgroup` and a
`cgroup.conf` constraining cores on every node, `none` writes back `slurm-setup.sh`'s
`task/none` and `proctrack/linuxproc` and removes `cgroup.conf`; each restarts the daemons and
ends with a job's two tasks' allowed CPUs. The runbook's §5 says how H26 runs under it. Tried on
the `osoe` VM's one-node Slurm (24.11.5, cgroup v2, `slurmd` with `Delegate=yes`): `site` bound
the two tasks to CPUs 0 and 1, `none` gave both 0-3, and the VM's own `slurm.conf`
(`task/affinity`) was put back byte for byte, Slurm idle and running jobs after. 125 items.
