## 1. `mix`: the design question, settled (Claude)

The brief's question: symmetric allocation is collective over the world
(§9.3), and every exercise allocates in its `setup`, so no exercise can set
up on one team while another team runs. It suggested splitting `setup` into
a world-collective allocation and a team-collective start.

- **One hook, `rebind`, rather than four.** An exercise's `setup` stays what
  it was - it allocates for the world and starts on the team it is given -
  and ends by calling its `rebind`, which moves the set-up exercise onto a
  team afresh: its view of the team, its counters, the period's seed, a sync.
  `mix` sets every exercise up on the world, then calls `rebind` with each
  period's team, and `rebind` with the world before `teardown`. The plain
  path does not change, and an exercise without `rebind` stays out of `mix`.
  *Undo*: the brief's `allocate`/`begin`/`end`/`release`, which splits every
  exercise in four where this adds one function to each.
- **Teams**: K strided teams of the world, `k, k + K, ...` - 4 from 8 PEs, 2
  from 4, else 1, or `--mix-teams` - every spec team being strided. At 4 × 4
  each team has one PE on every node, so every team crosses every link.
- **Periods**: in team period q, team k runs `mailbox`, `atomics`, `rma`,
  `locks` or `collectives`, the one at (k + q) mod 5, distinct across the
  teams; each team's period lasts half to one and a half `--period` (10 s by
  default), drawn from the seed, so the teams' steps and checkpoints fall out
  of step; every fourth period the world runs `sort` or `alltoall` in turn; a
  world sync ends every period. `--seconds` is the whole run.
- **Left out**: `memory`, whose routines are the world's; `teams`, which
  splits its own teams from its budget of numbers; `threads`, which needs
  `SHMEM_THREAD_MULTIPLE`; `print`; `deprecated`, the world's alone; and
  `waits`, which has a `rebind` and could join (For the author, 1).
- **Memory**: each of the seven exercises of a mix gets a seventh of the
  budget.
- **Plants fire inside `mix`**: `mix-locks-count` asserts `locks-count`'s
  line from team `mix1`'s period.

## 2. What the first soak found: the tool's own hang (Claude)

The Pi's first soak of `mix` hung seven minutes in, with every PE asleep
(load 0.00 on every node). `osoe-stat` on each node said where: each node's
first PE blocked in `shmem_uint64_sum_reduce` at epoch 83, its second in
`shmem_uint64_xor_reduce` at 84, the other two in `shmem_barrier_all` at 84,
for 329 s. PEs in different collectives at one epoch is a program whose PEs
called different collectives: `collectives`, run by `mix` on a team of four,
timed `shmem_barrier_all` - the whole job's - among its timing cases; the
four PEs' barrier was matched by the other twelve's sync at the period's end,
so the job fell one world collective out of step, and later hung in `sort`'s
reductions. The fix: the barrier's timing runs only on the world. Nothing
else a team exercise calls is the whole job's; their allocations are all
made at setup, on the world.

The quick `mix` had never reached that case - the last of 87 - because every
period started every exercise at round 0 again. So **`atomics`, `rma`,
`locks` and `collectives` now carry their rounds from period to period**,
alike on a team's members, which run a team's periods together; `rma` sets
its halo flag, which counts rounds, to the rounds done when it moves;
`mailbox` keeps its reset, its signal words being counts of its own. The
test `mix-cycle` runs 240 periods of three rounds, taking `collectives` past
all its cases on a team: 6.9 s on the Pi's login node, and with the
barrier's guard removed it hangs. *Undo*: the reset in each `rebind`.

**No library failure was found** - the brief's first stop - in this phase.
But the hang showed a gap the README had papered over:
`OSOE_NO_PROGRESS_TIMEOUT` watches only the library's own progress (design
§3.15), so a program's own deadlock - a barrier one PE never enters, a wait
nobody satisfies - waits for ever with it set; tried at 5 s, on one node and
across two, the launcher's 30 s ran out first. That is as designed; the
README now says so, and the question is the author's (For the author, 3).

## 3. The library's counters in the line (Claude)

- **Read by each node's first PE**, from its node's control segment, through
  `tools/stat`'s reader, which finds every field by name: the segment is the
  library's, `/osoe.<job>.<step>.<node>.0`, its name gone once the job is up
  and found through this process's own descriptor.
- **Only at the world's checkpoints**: a team's would count only the nodes
  whose first PE is in it. So in `mix` a world line's growth covers the team
  periods before it.
- **Left out, not guessed**, where the segment cannot be opened or a field is
  not in it; the test `library` asserts the line appears, both nodes' and
  with bytes sent, so a reader that stopped finding it would show.

## 4. `--fold-nodes` (Claude)

- **A group folds only when whole**: when it holds every PE of each host it
  touches. Rank 0 decides a group at a time from a tally against each host's
  PEs, which init counts: O(k log k) for a group of k. The fold falls back to
  PE numbers where the names would outgrow the room PE numbers take.
- **A host is a host name**, as `gethostname` gives it up to its first dot:
  `osoe-launch`'s logical nodes on one machine fold together.
- **Checked whatever the flag says**: the `print` exercise runs four folded
  cases - a line a host, each host's last PE apart (which must keep PE
  numbers), every PE alike, `SHMEM_TEAM_SHARED` - and folds host lists
  written out by hand on every PE, which `print-fold` fires; a plant test
  with the flag asserts a failure every PE of the one host reports comes
  labeled by the host.
- **The driver's `hosts=`** folds through the same routine, now O(N) where it
  was O(N × nodes).

## 5. The second route of `parallel_print` (Claude)

- **Dissemination, not recursive doubling**: in round k each member puts the
  records it holds of the 2^k ranks from its own on to the member 2^k before
  it, with `shmem_putmem_signal` adding one to a signal word of the round's
  own, and waits for the member 2^k after; ⌈log₂ n⌉ rounds for any n. A word
  a round, since rounds' puts come from different members and land in any
  order. The proposal's (hash, lowest rank, count) lists could not give the
  labels, which need every member; the records are what the fcollect gives.
- **The differential is the `print` exercise's**: every case by both routes
  against one prediction; `--print-route signal` takes a whole run's lines by
  the new route. `print-route` changes PE 1's record on the signal route
  alone, and only that route's checks fire.

## 6. The families phase 3's shim left uncalled (Claude)

- **`put_signal`**: two classes of `rma` over every kind, after the first 140
  cases so that their plants keep their rounds - 198 cases, 792 rounds a
  cycle. The receiver waits on the signal and reads its inbox then, before
  any sync, so data a signal overtook shows. The halo gains `putmem_signal`
  and its `_nbi`, which raise the flag themselves.
- **`waits`**, in the default set: every form over the standard AMO types,
  which since 1.5 are the types a wait takes, each word written by another
  member's atomic set; all six comparisons, starting the words at 0 or 101;
  the indices an `_any` or `_some` form returns checked, and the returns with
  every word excluded. The two-byte types, which no AMO takes, are
  `deprecated`'s.
- **The single routines where they carry weight**: the byte forms of the
  collectives among `collectives`' typed cases, `alltoallsmem` with them;
  `shmem_sync_all` as `mix`'s period-ending sync in odd periods;
  `shmem_pe_accessible` in `memory`'s check; blocking `getmem` in turn with
  `getmem_nbi` in `alltoall`; `shmem_query_initialized` before init and
  after; `shmem_pcontrol(1)`.
- **The trap of item 8 of the afternoon, twice more**: `alltoall`'s get
  variant alternated on the exchange number, whose parity the eight variants
  fix, so blocking `getmem` was never called; and `waits` first laid its
  words 8 bytes apart, which the many-word forms, taking an array of the
  type, read as other words - the first run hung, and a count, again, is
  what showed the first.

## 7. The deprecated forms (Claude)

- **`deprecated`**, run only when named, on the world: the 44 `_to_all`
  reductions against a sequential fold of the members' inputs; broadcast,
  collect, fcollect, alltoall and alltoalls of 32 and 64 bits; the
  active-set barrier and sync, each member counting at the set's first PE,
  whole when the call returns; the 30 old atomic names, by name and by the
  deprecated generics, their returns and the words after; the old waits; the
  cache routines. The collectives run on the whole job, the even PEs, the odd
  ones and the upper half in turn, and a PE outside the set must find its
  dest as it was. 92 cases; 368 rounds take each on every set in both
  spellings. Seven plants.
- **The two-byte waits by a put**: `short` and `unsigned short` have no AMO,
  and 1.5 requires a waited word be written by one (change #17); they are
  written as the contract they were deprecated with allowed.
- **`osoe-pacer-start-pes`**: `start_pes` starts the library where
  `shmem_init` would and ends it at exit, so it has a program of its own;
  `_my_pe` and `_num_pes`, `shmalloc`, `shrealloc`, `shmemalign` and
  `shfree`, each checked, with four plants and a counted build.
- **`shmem_wait` is a macro too**: the plain spelling's generic reached
  `shmem_long_wait`; the function is called as `(shmem_wait)(...)`.
