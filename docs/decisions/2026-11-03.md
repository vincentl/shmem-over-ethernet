# 2026-11-03: the four design changes' sixteen questions answered

## The ask

The four design changes the large-cluster playbook names - C5, the intranode
combine sliced over the node's members; C8, the two-level alltoall; C9, the
hierarchical prefix scan; C17, the control tree - were modelled on 2026-11-02
at the author's answer 6 of [2026-11-01](2026-11-01.md), each with a proposal
under `docs/design/` putting four questions to the author with a
recommendation each (`2026-11-02-intranode-combine-model.md`,
`2026-11-02-two-level-alltoall-model.md`,
`2026-11-02-hierarchical-scan-model.md`,
`2026-11-02-control-tree-model.md`). The author read the four and, on the
morning of 2026-11-03, took every recommendation as his answer: "Take the
recommendations".

## The answers

Each question as its proposal numbers it; the answer is the recommendation,
restated so that this record stands alone.

### C5, the intranode combine (`intranode-combine-proposal.md` §7)

1. **Built after the first cluster session's PEs-a-node rung at 64 PEs**,
   here, for the second session. The VM cannot time the change past four
   cores, and the suite tests the protocol it changes everywhere.
2. **The threshold is a fixed rule**: slices of at least 1 KiB, the serial
   combine below L × 1 KiB; a setting only if the ladder's numbers ask for
   one.
3. **The arrival's wake is wake-all**, as the one-node release does, and
   measured.
4. **The release's O(L) wake is measured in the same run** and changed only if
   it shows.

### C8, the two-level alltoall (`two-level-alltoall-proposal.md` §6)

1. **Built after the ladder's alltoall row at 64 nodes** and before the second
   session, here, the wire's version bumped with whatever else that stretch
   changes; the model's message cost at many peers is its least certain term.
2. **The schedule's order is the node order**, (s + k) mod n from k = 1,
   measured on the ladder against the as-built start, since the model cannot
   price the incast.
3. **A member's message is chunked as a put's parts**, no new rule.
4. **One change, both forms**: `alltoall` and the strided `alltoalls` take
   the same message.

### C9, the hierarchical scan (`hierarchical-scan-proposal.md` §6)

1. **Built only if scans come to matter** - the ladder's scan row at 1 and P
   PEs a node, or a program the author means to run; otherwise it stays
   modelled, the least of the four by use.
2. **Both classes, one change**, since the pipeline over the leads' tree is
   the reductions' and the member scan's already.
3. **A scan spanning nodes takes an epoch**, with P24's check; `osoe-stat`'s
   count and the tests' pins move with it.
4. **C5's slices come to the scan's two passes after C5 is in** on the
   reduction; the scan takes its code, not its own.

### C17, the control tree (`control-tree-proposal.md` §6)

1. **Built after the first cluster session's start-up curve and `ss -tm`
   reads at 512 nodes and above**, and before any run past about 2,000 nodes,
   where the connection buffers' budget halves and refuses at 4,097.
2. **The other trees' wires are made at the first call that needs them**, the
   data wires' rule, one mechanism.
3. **The second notice on a control wire is the same message kind**, with a
   wire version bump; no kind of its own.
4. **A dead node's far side is accepted**: the lowest caller's node may leave
   before the nodes beyond a dead neighbour; the job is a failure and its
   status 1 either way.

## What the answers fix

- **The order is C5, C8, C17, C9**, each gated on the first cluster session's
  ladder: C5 on the 64 PEs-a-node rung, C8 on the alltoall row at 64 nodes,
  C17 on the start-up curve and `ss -tm` at 512 nodes and above, C9 on the
  scan row or a program that needs it. C5, C8 and C17 are built here between
  the first session and the second; C9 only if its gate opens.
- **Nothing is built before the first session**, so the ladder times the code
  as it is; the playbook's §5 rows carry the decided gates in their status
  cells, and each proposal's questions section says the answers are taken.
- **Each change carries its measurement**: C5's wakes, C8's schedule order
  against the as-built start, so that what the models could not price is
  measured once the change runs.

## How to undo

A line in `docs/decisions/README.md` under "Scope and goals" names the four
and their gates; to drop or reorder a change, amend that line and the
proposal's questions section, and say so in a dated record.
