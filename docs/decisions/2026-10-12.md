## H21 on the Pi, that night

The night's plan (the author's, step 1): Träff's cost model optimistic against
the Pi from 256 KiB and `osoe-bench`'s 64 KiB reduction back to back no faster,
both from step 3, measured again on `main` at `02a7944` with the existing tools

and bound at no helpers it loses 0.8 ms a call while the trees lose nothing - a
matter for a site's binding (B11, H26). H21 is closed; 168 items.
