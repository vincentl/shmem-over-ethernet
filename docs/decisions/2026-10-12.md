the order of the work's second step), from about 01:15 to 02:30 UTC, the author

## The machines

Eight `c4-standard-16` and a head node, `slurm-controller` (`e2-standard-4`), made
by the author's Terraform (his file, not in the tree) in `us-central1-a`: Debian
13, gVNIC, the VPC's MTU 8896, a compact placement policy, OS Login, IAP for every
`ssh` and no external address, and the TCP settings of a site tuning its nodes.

(32) and its C4 family's in the region (24), both raised. The second was why the
first apply placed one node, which the lead took at first for the compact
placement's capacity; the placement held once the quota allowed eight. The
lead's advice to turn `automatic_restart` on, which Compute Engine refuses for a
collocated placement, is corrected in 2026-10-11's record.
